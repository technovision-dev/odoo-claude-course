# FAQ

### Does it work with several companies?
Yes. Levels, settings and the log are per company, with record rules, and a quotation only
uses the levels of its own company.

### The scheduled action did not run for a week. Will customers get several emails?
No. Each quotation gets only the highest level it has reached, once.

### What counts as the customer answering?
A message on the quotation written by the customer or anyone from the customer's company, on
or after the day it was sent: an email reply that reaches the chatter, or a portal comment.
Notes by your own team do not count.

### Does re-sending a quotation restart the follow-ups?
No. Re-sending a quotation that is already in "Quotation Sent" keeps its first sent date, and a
level already sent to it is never sent again.

### What happens if an email cannot be generated?
The log records "Failed" with the reason, and the other quotations are still processed. Fix
the cause and use **Send follow-up now**.

### Who receives the email?
The quotation's customer, in the customer's language.

### What happens when I update the module?
Your levels, settings, and your edits to the included templates and the scheduled action are
kept.

### Does it follow up on expired quotations?
Not by default. Turn off **Skip expired quotations** in Settings to include them.
